About us

Tradition in safe hands

Thai Most is a Thai massage salon in Most, which first opened its doors in 2004. Since then, we have built up a prestigious reputation and are proud to have a large number of loyal clients who have been visiting us regularly ever since we first opened.

The massages are performed exclusively by native Thai or Filipino women who are certified by the prestigious temple school Wat Pho in Thailand.

The foundation of all massages is Thai massage – Nuat phaen boran, นวดแผนโบราณ. This approach to working with the body combines elements of acupressure and yoga. Due to the large number of stretching manoeuvres involved, it is often referred to as ‘applied hatha yoga’ or ‘yoga for the lazy’.

Frequently Asked Questions

Thai massages in Most – answers to your questions

How much does a Thai massage cost in Most?

A traditional Thai massage at Thai Most starts at 790 CZK for 60 minutes (90 mins 1,100 CZK, 120 mins 1,600 CZK). A back and neck massage or a head and neck massage starts at 430 CZK for 30 minutes. The full price list for all 19 massages and treatments is available further up the page.

How do I book a massage?

The quickest way is to call +420 778 429 329 (Mon–Fri 9 am–8 pm), or use the ‘Book online’ form or WhatsApp – reception will confirm your appointment. With five female massage therapists and three treatment rooms, we can often find you an appointment on the same day.

Do you offer couples’ massage and spa treatments?

Yes. The couples’ massage takes place in a double treatment room with two therapists working simultaneously. You’ll have a private whirlpool and sauna all to yourselves – available separately from 400 CZK, or as the finale to your couples’ massage (120-minute couples’ massage package + champagne + whirlpool + sauna: 2,600 CZK).

How does a gift voucher work?

We can issue a voucher for any amount or for a specific massage. You can order it online on this page, collect it from the salon, or we can send it to you as a PDF by email. It is valid for all massages, treatments and wellness services; the recipient can book an appointment by telephone.

What is a traditional Thai massage and how does it work?

Thai massage (nuat phaen boran) combines acupressure, work on energy meridians and stretching in yoga poses. It is performed without oil, in comfortable clothing, on a mattress. The masseuses at Thai Most are native Thai and Filipino women certified by the Wat Pho school.

How does the ‘every sixth massage free’ bonus work?

With your loyalty card, you’ll receive one free massage from us after every fifth massage you’ve paid for. You can check your stamp balance online in the Bonus Programme section.
